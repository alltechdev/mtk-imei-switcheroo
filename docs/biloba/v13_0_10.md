# V13.0.10.0.SCUMIXM findings

Analyzed 2026-10-12. Offline verification passed; no hardware boot test.
Inputs are the modem, LK and preloader from the supplied Android 12 firmware ZIP.
See [fingerprints](artifacts.md#v130100scumixm), [patcher commands](patchers.md#v130100-pair)
and [full reproduction](reproduce.md).

## Modem identification

The `ro.boot.hwlevel` string is at ROM `0x015237d0`, with a literal-pool pointer
at `0x0130d87c`. The decoded gate at `0x0130d700` references both that property
and the name-bearing `is_need_enable_critical_data_check` log string, and compares
product names including biloba. The caller starts at `0x0130d904`, calls the gate
at `0x0130d92a`, and branches on a nonzero return at `0x0130d930`.

The six-byte return-zero stub goes at complete-image offset `0x0130d900`.
The unchanged caller then frees its allocation, takes the skip path and returns 1.
Original bytes are `f0 64 20 6e 0c 04`; replacement is `00 6a a0 e8 00 65`.
This is MIPS16 code, not Thumb.

## LK and preloader

The V13 LK `md1img` policy entry starts at `0x0012225c`; byte `0x00122273`
changes from `02` to `00`. The selector at runtime `0x4c417920` extracts bit 1
from the policy selected by secure-boot and lock state. The modem verifier at
`0x4c455bf8` reads the selected flag at `0x4c607024`. The authentication call
reaches `0x4c46cca8` when required.

The bundled preloader's LK policy is `01 00 03 00` at file `0x4bfa4`.
Its selector at `0x22fee4` returns zero for unlocked states in the isolated tests.
The preloader is never patched. Matching V12's LK edit offset does not imply
that the LK images are interchangeable; their fingerprints differ.

## Verification

Two MIPS caller cases pass, including the deliberately wrong expectation
that must fail. Fifteen ARM cases cover the original/patched LK and preloader
selectors plus authentication reachability: original unlocked and patched locked
reach the endpoint; patched unlocked skips it. External state providers and the
authentication endpoint are stubbed, and LTE/C2K flags are clear.

Original modem certificate signatures verify. The patched signed ROM digest
differs as expected; signature bytes remain unchanged. Output sizes are identical
to the originals, with exactly six modem bytes and one LK byte changed.
The supplied key archive contains no matching key for the V13 modem certificates.

[Saved disassembly and execution evidence](../../research/biloba/v13/evidence)
is separate from firmware inputs and outputs. Hardware modem authentication,
accepted unlock state and complete boot remain device-test requirements.
